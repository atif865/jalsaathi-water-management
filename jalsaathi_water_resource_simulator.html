05 / Physical model: build it from reused materials

1. Rain collection

Make a sloped cardboard roof or funnel and direct water into a collection cup.

2. Filter layer

Use a cut bottle with gravel and sand to demonstrate filtration. Do not drink the output.

3. Storage tank

Use a transparent reused bottle, marked at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.

4. Distribution

Use spare straws/tubing and a measuring cup to demonstrate supply to a model house.

How to present the physical model with the website

Select Drought Alert on the website and lower the bottle water to the matching level. Select Flood Risk and pour a little extra water into the collection area (use a tray to catch spills). Select Water Pollution to explain why visually clear water is not necessarily safe. Select Rainwater Harvesting and pour clean water through your collection funnel into the tank. These are manual demonstrations—the website does not automatically read the bottle.
